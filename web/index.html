OCO Reactive Orders

Stop-loss, take-profit and trailing-stop orders on a Uniswap v4 pool. A contract on Reactive Network watches every swap and asks the vault to sell when a level is crossed; no off-chain bot. Testnets only (Unichain Sepolia + Reactive Lasna), test tokens.
